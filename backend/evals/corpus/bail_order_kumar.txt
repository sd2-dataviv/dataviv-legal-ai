IN THE HIGH COURT OF DELHI AT NEW DELHI

BAIL APPLN. 1234/2026

STATE versus VIKRAM KUMAR

CORAM: HON'BLE MR. JUSTICE A. KUMAR

ORDER
12.03.2026

1. The applicant seeks regular bail in FIR No. 245/2025 registered at Police Station Saket under Section 318 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023.

2. The applicant has been in custody since 2 December 2025. The chargesheet has been filed and the trial is likely to take time.

3. The applicant is admitted to regular bail on furnishing a personal bond of Rs. 50,000 with one surety of the like amount to the satisfaction of the Trial Court, subject to the following conditions:
(a) the applicant shall not leave the National Capital Region without prior permission of the Trial Court;
(b) the applicant shall surrender his passport before the Trial Court;
(c) the applicant shall report to the Investigating Officer every Monday at 10:00 AM.

4. List for compliance on 15.04.2026.
