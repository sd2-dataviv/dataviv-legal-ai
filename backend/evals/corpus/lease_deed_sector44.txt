LEASE DEED

This Lease Deed is executed at Gurugram on 25 March 2026 between Mr. Suresh Iyer (the "Lessor") and Brightpath Learning Private Limited (the "Lessee").

This Lease Deed is duly stamped. Stamp duty of Rs. 1,73,000 has been paid vide e-stamp certificate number IN-HR12345678.

1. PREMISES
1.1 The Lessor lets to the Lessee the office premises at Plot 14, Sector 44, Gurugram, Haryana, admeasuring 6,000 square feet.

2. TERM
2.1 The lease is for a term of three years commencing on 1 April 2026.
2.2 The first twelve (12) months of the term are a lock-in period during which neither party may terminate this Lease.

3. RENT AND DEPOSIT
3.1 The Lessee shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 2,40,000 on or before the 7th day of each month.
3.2 The rent shall increase by 5% on each anniversary of the commencement date.
3.3 The Lessee has paid an interest-free refundable security deposit of Rs. 14,40,000.

4. TERMINATION
4.1 After the lock-in period, either party may terminate this Lease by giving three months written notice.

5. JURISDICTION
5.1 This Lease is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Gurugram shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
