SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Share Purchase Agreement is made at New Delhi on 1 March 2026 between Lotus Holdings Private Limited, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 (the "Seller"), and Zenith Ventures LLP, a limited liability partnership (the "Buyer").

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Completion" means completion of the sale and purchase of the Sale Shares under clause 4.
1.2 "Sale Shares" means 10,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10 each of Lotus Retail Private Limited.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE
2.1 The Seller shall sell and the Buyer shall purchase the Sale Shares free from all Encumbrances.

3. CONSIDERATION
3.1 The consideration for the Sale Shares is Rs. 12,50,00,000 (Rupees Twelve Crore Fifty Lakh only), payable by the Buyer at Completion by RTGS.

4. COMPLETION
4.1 Completion shall take place within 30 days of the date of this Agreement.

9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
9.1 The aggregate liability of the Seller under this Agreement shall not exceed Rs. 2,00,00,000 (Rupees Two Crore only).

10. CONFIDENTIALITY
10.1 Each party shall keep the terms of this Agreement confidential for a period of five years from Completion.

12. TERMINATION
12.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving thirty (30) days written notice to the other party.
12.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect if the other party commits a material breach which is not remedied within 15 days of written notice.

14. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES
14.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.
14.2 Any dispute shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat of arbitration shall be New Delhi.

15. STAMP DUTY
15.1 The Buyer shall bear the stamp duty payable on this Agreement and on the transfer of the Sale Shares.
